Happy Girlfriend's Day, Baby ❤️

I don't think I'll ever be able to thank you enough for coming into my life. Sometimes I just sit quietly and think about us, and honestly, I feel so lucky. Out of all the people in this world, somehow I got you. Aur believe me, that's something I'll never take for granted.

Thank you for every little effort you take for me. Thank you for listening to my endless bakwaas, for making time for me even when you're busy, for understanding me on days when even I don't understand myself, and for loving me in ways that you probably don't even realize. Teri har chhoti si effort mere liye bohot badi hoti hai. Shayad mai har baar express nahi kar pata, but I notice everything, and I appreciate all of it more than you'll ever know.

I know the past few days weren't the easiest for us. Communication kabhi kabhi thoda difficult ho gaya, hum dono thode frustrated bhi hue, aur chhoti chhoti misunderstandings bhi hui. But baby, mujhe ek cheez ka hamesha confidence tha. Yeh sirf ek phase tha. Humara love kabhi kam nahi hua. Din ke end pe, no matter kitna bhi argue kare ya kitna bhi upset ho jaaye, I still look at you and think, "Yahi toh meri ladki hai." And that's enough for me to keep fighting for us.

You know what's my favourite thing about us? We don't give up. We try. We talk. We learn. We make mistakes, we realise them, and we become a little better than yesterday. Mujhe kabhi tera perfect version nahi chahiye tha, because mai khud bhi perfect nahi hu. Mujhe bas tera real version chahiye. The one who laughs too much, gets angry sometimes, overthinks, loves deeply, and still chooses me every single day.

When I think about my future, I don't just think about getting a good job or becoming successful. I think about you being there for all of it. I want to build my life with you. I want us to work hard together, celebrate every achievement together, and support each other through every failure. I want to see you become everything you've ever dreamed of, and I want to be the loudest person clapping for you when you do.

One day, I want to marry you. I want to stand there, look at you, and know that after everything life threw at us, we still found our way to each other. I want to build a home with you, fill it with laughter, random arguments over silly things, late-night talks, and so much love that it never feels empty. I want to have kids with you someday and tell them how their mom has been my favourite person long before they were even born. I want them to grow up watching us choose each other every single day.

Aur ek din, jab mai tujhe apni family ke saath dekhunga na, I won't just introduce you. I'll be proud. Proud that the girl standing beside me is the woman I love with all my heart. Proud that I get to call you mine. Proud that I found someone as caring, kind and beautiful as you.

Thank you for accepting me with all my flaws. Thank you for staying even when loving me wasn't always easy. Thank you for believing in us. Thank you for making my ordinary days feel special just by being a part of them.

Baby, I don't know what the future has planned for us. Life will test us, we'll probably have more misunderstandings, more stressful days and more moments where things don't go the way we want. But if there's one thing I'm absolutely sure about, it's this... I never want to face any of it without you.

So on this Girlfriend's Day, I don't just want to say that I love you. I want to say that I'm grateful for you. I'm proud of you. I appreciate you. And no matter how much time passes, I'll keep choosing you, respecting you, growing with you, and loving you with the same heart that fell for you.

Happy Girlfriend's Day, my baby. ❤️

I love you today, I'll love you tomorrow, and I'll keep finding new reasons to love you for the rest of my life.

Forever yours,Avadhut ❤️
